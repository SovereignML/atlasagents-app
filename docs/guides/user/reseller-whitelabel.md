# Reseller / white-label

> Run SovereignML under your own brand, on your own domain, and bill your own customers directly.

Related: [Billing & credits](billing-credits.md), [Teams & collaboration](teams.md).

---

> **Enterprise / white-label only.** The reseller panel appears only for teams with the white-label entitlement. If you don't see a **Reseller** entry in your nav, contact sales to enable it.

## What white-label gives you

Your customers sign up on **your** domain, see **your** branding, pay **your** Stripe account, and their agents run on **your** LLM account. SovereignML is invisible to them — you're the vendor. You manage all of it from the **Reseller** panel.

## One-time setup checklist

The reseller **Dashboard** shows a setup-readiness checklist. Complete these in **Branding & Settings**:

1. **Branding & custom domain** — upload your logo, set colors, and connect your custom dashboard domain. Customers on that domain see your brand throughout.
2. **Stripe Connect** — connect your Stripe account so customers pay **you** directly (SovereignML takes no cut of your customer revenue).
3. **Your OpenRouter key** — connect your own LLM account so your customers' agents run on your OpenRouter credits (you carry their LLM cost and price it as you like).
4. **Your Chatwoot** — connect your own Chatwoot so customer support tickets and live chat land in **your** inbox.
5. **Plans** — define your own plans with your own USD prices. Each creates a price on your Stripe account, and its limits apply to the customer who subscribes.

## Running the business

The Reseller panel is grouped into Overview / Customers / Operations / Finance / Configuration:

| Area | What you do |
|------|-------------|
| **Dashboard** | Customer counts, running agents, an account-health view (healthy / at-risk / churn-risk), and the setup checklist. |
| **Customers** | Your end-customer accounts (they self-register on your domain). Suspend / unsuspend any customer. |
| **Users** | Every user across customer teams; send a password reset when a customer is locked out. |
| **Agents** | Every agent across all customers. **Manage as customer** (impersonate) to operate a customer's agent and fix issues directly. |
| **Backups** | Read-only overview of backups across customers. |
| **Notifications** | Read-only activity feed across customer teams. |
| **Support Inbox** | Answer customer conversations from your own Chatwoot. |
| **Subscriptions** | Customer subscriptions billed on your connected Stripe. |
| **Revenue** | Per-customer estimated compute spend (your cost view). |
| **Branding & Settings** | The four config blocks above (branding, support, billing/LLM, plans). |

## Notes for your customers

- End users on your custom domain see your brand end-to-end.
- On a custom domain, the login page shows **email/password** only — social sign-in (Google/X/Apple) stays on the canonical domain.
- If your team ever drops below the white-label entitlement, dashboards revert to the default look; your saved branding is kept and restored if you re-upgrade.
