# Teams & collaboration

> Invite teammates, assign roles, share agents, and manage seats, SSH keys, and integrations.

Related: [Managing your agent](managing-agents.md), [Billing & credits](billing-credits.md).

---

## Your team

Every account has a team. The owner always occupies one **seat**; your plan sets how many seats you have (from 1 on Free to unlimited on Enterprise — see [Billing & credits](billing-credits.md)). Manage everything from the **Team** page.

## Inviting members

Invite by **email**. The invitee gets a link to join your team. Each accepted member takes one seat; you can't exceed your plan's seat limit without upgrading or buying capacity.

## Roles

Team members hold a role that sets what they can do across the workspace (for example viewer, developer/operator, manager, owner). On top of that, **each agent can be shared individually** at viewer / operator / manager level from the agent's **Access** tab — so you can give someone full control of one agent and read-only on another. See [Managing your agent → Access & sharing](managing-agents.md#access--sharing).

## SSH keys

Add and manage SSH keys (with fingerprints) from the **Team** page or the dedicated **SSH Keys** page. These are used for server access where applicable.

## Integrations & backup providers

The Team area is also where you connect shared **integrations** and **backup providers** (such as Google Drive for agent backups) so they're available to the whole team rather than a single user.

## Audit log

Every action taken on your account is recorded in the **Audit Log** (retained for 7 days), with filtering and export — handy for teams that need to see who changed what.
